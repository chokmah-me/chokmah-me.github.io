🔎 THE PROBLEM

Legal systems are rule systems. Open texture (“reasonable,” “undue burden”) is a design feature for judges — and search space for an optimizer.

Hart’s penumbra is the attack surface. Schmitt’s exception names who fills it. Repair still runs on a legislative clock.

KEY INSIGHT

⚡ RATE, NOT REFUSAL

AI collapses discovery cost. Repair does not.

Not a better system prompt. The asset at risk is the stock of known-but-unpatched ambiguities. Discovery and execution are two different problems.

📐 DEFINITION (not a fitted model)

G = Rd / Rp   ·   dS/dt = Rd − Rp
