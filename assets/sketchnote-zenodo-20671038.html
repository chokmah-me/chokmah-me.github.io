VALID AND FALSE SNAPPING IN EML TREES

The basin selection problem · symbolic regression

Daniyel Yaacov Bilar · Chokmah LLC · June 2026

Committed is not correct.

PROBLEM

Train EML (exp-minus-ln) expression trees, then snap soft input selectors to discrete choices. Commitment-based success looks green while the recovered formula is wrong.

KEY INSIGHT

INSIGHT

Add a symbolic-correctness criterion. Valid snaps recover the elementary form. False snaps are discrete, confident, and algebraically wrong. Depth that is too generous opens extra basins.

METHOD

Gradient descent, then argmax snap. Three-phase annealing (Adam on MAE, entropy-penalty ramp, temperature anneal) vs targeted warm-start + top-aligned curriculum.

FORMAL CORE

valid ⇔ snapped tree = target elementary form

RESULTS

Warm-start + curriculum recover valid exp and ln at over-representational depth, where annealing alone does not. Data: snapping_v2_final.csv and the basin-selection companion note.

LIMITS

iCE40 hardware is a separate note. Results are for exp/ln targets in the reported sweep, not a universal SR claim.
