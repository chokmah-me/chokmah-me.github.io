🧪 FALSIFICATION · simulate.py · three attack scenarios

A · Ghost clock
Stale-state injection. Δt ≤ −500 ms → 100% staleness bypass. Defense is authenticated t_auth, not a tighter TTL.
B · INT8 spoof
Quantization gap. Unit-scale zero-knowledge bypass ~0.06%. Raw sensor range (3–4 orders) widens the gap without normalization.
C · Lethal compliance
Mach 5–15, sub-10g, cold-start pop-in. 3a and 3b pass; Check 4 catches jumps above ~80 m. Sweet spot below that.

⚠️ LIMITS · Phase GD-0 is the gate

  • All thresholds (5 ms, 0.95 floor, 80 m coherence, 2σ swap gate) are engineering defaults, not hardware measurements.
  • Headline detection rates rest on parametric per-check models, not measured failure rates.
  • Feature normalization is a hard requirement for Check 3b; unit-scale sim understates the INT8 gap.
  • Residuals that stay: physics-compliant spoof below ~80 m · FLP under ISL jam · MIO authoring error · clock-trust under GPS+ISL denial.
