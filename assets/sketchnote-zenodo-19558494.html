A 92 percent drop in launch rate does not tell you whether command died or launchers did.

KEY INSIGHT

The campaign cut Iranian ballistic-missile launch rates 92 percent in nine days. Launch rate does not separate destroyed TELs from degraded command.

THREE C2 HYPOTHESES

H1
active distributed command
H2
pre-programmed execution
H3
mixed degradation

1,350 Monte Carlo runs. Launch-rate differences are non-significant (Mann-Whitney p > 0.05; rank-biserial r < 0.08).

WHAT DOES DISCRIMINATE

The emergent target ratio. It is not available from open sources.

r = 0.67, p < 0.001
