A STETHOSCOPE, NOT A TROPHY

Commentary · Willow 65-qubit OTOC

19 April 2026 · technical note

Independent-error models miss the correlation. The correlator is the instrument that hears it.

KEY INSIGHT · COMMENTARY

Google's OTOC experiments on the 65-qubit Willow processor (Nature 646, 825-830, 2025) are read here as a diagnostic for correlated noise, not only as a quantum-advantage benchmark.

TWO NUMBERS FROM THE NOTE

SNR 3.9 observed vs 0.4 independent-error model · χ²/ν ≈ 14.2
1−ρ ≈ 0.003 for OTOC(1) · 1−ρ ≈ 0.445 for OTOC(2)

Pauli insertion separates small-loop and large-loop interference. The note scores these against Kalai 2016 and 2023.

LIMIT

Commentary on Google's experiment and on the author's 2024 Willow note. This board does not claim a new device run.

HOW THE COMMENTARY SCORES THE EXPERIMENT

  • Independent-error model: SNR about 0.4. Observed SNR 3.9. The miss is about an order of magnitude. χ²/ν ≈ 14.2.
  • Pauli insertion: 1−ρ ≈ 0.003 on OTOC(1), small loop. 1−ρ ≈ 0.445 on OTOC(2), large loop.
  • Device: 65-qubit Willow. Venue of the experiment: Nature 646, 825-830 (2025).
  • Scored against Kalai 2016 and 2023. This note follows the author's 2024 Willow analysis.
