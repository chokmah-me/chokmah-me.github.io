🧩 THE FOUR OPERATIONAL GAPS

1
Hardware
HSMs, bootloader trust stores, RSA/ECC ASICs. Refresh, not a patch.
2
Handshake
kB-scale ML-KEM/ML-DSA vs tens of bytes. Extra RTT past one packet. PQ certs are the heavy half.
3
Vendors
Named algs, hybrid mode, agility, CBOM, FIPS 140-3 + CAVP IDs. “Quantum-safe” is a red flag.
4
Discovery
Cron, mesh, OT, pinned agents. Generate a CBOM and diff it. Inventory sets duration.
