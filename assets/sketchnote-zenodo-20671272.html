WARM-START BEATS ANNEALING ALONE

exp and ln at over-representational depth

12 June 2026 · data and methods note

Three-phase annealing does not recover valid exp and ln at that depth. A targeted warm-start and a top-aligned curriculum do.

KEY INSIGHT

Full valid recovery for exp and ln, including at over-representational depth, comes from a targeted warm-start plus a top-aligned curriculum.
