LATTICEOS ON THE AOM

Schema, counter-narrative, and implementation

16 August 2026 · software and notes · v4.1

The governance gap is tenant observability, not the message bus.

KEY INSIGHT

LatticeOS is mapped onto a four-layer Autonomous Operations Model. The failure that matters is automation complacency (OWASP ASI09): operators stop checking a bus that looks healthy.

FOUR LAYERS

Sense
what the edge sees
Decide
what the node may do
Act
the kinetic or cyber effect
Account
who can see the tenant

Account is the layer the bus does not provide. A tenant that cannot observe its own authorizations is governed in name only.

WHERE COMPLACENCY SITS

Healthy busmessages move
→
Quiet tenantno local check
→
ASI09operator trusts the pipe

CLAIM

Schema plus counter-narrative. This record is the mapping, not a live Lattice deployment log.

LIMIT

No measured intercept timeline and no customer telemetry. Complacency is the named failure mode, not a counted incident rate.

WHAT EACH LAYER MUST SHOW

Sensethe edge report the tenant can audit
Decidethe check that runs before an effect
Actthe effect the bus actually carried
Accounta tenant log that is not the bus log

If Account is only the bus log, the tenant cannot see its own authorizations. That is the gap this mapping names.

COUNTER-NARRATIVE IN ONE LINE

A healthy message bus is not evidence of governance. Complacency (OWASP ASI09) is what you get when operators treat bus health as authorization health.
