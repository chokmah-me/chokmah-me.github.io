Below the Masking Threshold: Observable Command-and-Control Discrimination in a Residual IRGC Missile Force

Bilar, Daniyel Yaacov · 2026-08-10 · publication/preprint · cc-by-4.0

Download PDF (Zenodo): open

Abstract

Artifact claim. Phase II companion preprint (v0.2): in a residual IRGC missile force after high week-one attrition, an open-source attribution-correlation proxy can discriminate active from pre-programmed C2 under sparse provocation; dense provocation saturates that proxy (H_ceiling). Context. The parent study found that under high launcher attrition, daily launch rate cannot distinguish C2 architectures; only the emergent target ratio discriminates, and it is unobservable from open sources (paper concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.19210451; version 10.5281/zenodo.19558494). Phase II tests the residual-force boundary: low attrition with sparse provocation (April–June 2026) versus dense provocation after fighting resumed in July 2026. Contents. This deposit is the PDF only (PHASE2_C2_Observability_REL_FIXED.pdf). Simulation code, CSV outputs, and figure scripts are under the separate software concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.19210120 (repository chokmah-me/milcom-2026-iran-attrition). Paper PDF and software zip are distinct Zenodo concepts. Reproduce (pointer). The PDF is not executable. Software-side: python verify_milcom_claims.py; Phase II grid simulation/phase2_runner.py; Leg 3 null floor simulation/leg3_null_sim.py. Leg 3 pre-registration: 10.5281/zenodo.21443990. Limitations / non-claims. Leg 2 Stream B is small-n (no inferential test on the empirical rate alone). Attribution correlation is a proxy, not equivalence to the emergent target ratio. Near-perfect simulation separation at high connectivity reflects pure H1/H2 archetypes. Under dense July provocation the construct hits H_ceiling and is not a standing C2 indicator. Not a claim of MILCOM acceptance. This version (v0.2). Adds Leg 3 out-of-sample test under resumed hostilities, Leg 2 retro-audit of coincidence structure, H_ceiling interpretation (saturation plus unattributable-antecedent deflation), and count-corrected release narrative.
