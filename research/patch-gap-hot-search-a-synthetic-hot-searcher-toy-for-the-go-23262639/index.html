patch-gap-hot-search: a synthetic HOT-searcher toy for the governance patch-gap

Bilar, Daniyel Yaacov · 2026-10-09 · software · mit-license

Version of record (canonical): https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23262639

Abstract

1. Artifact claim. This deposit is a Python, stdlib-only toy that generates a discovery rate Rd from a label-blind interface searcher on a Highly Optimized Tolerance (HOT) rule graph; the patch-gap G = Rd/Rp and the stocks Sknown, Sactive are outputs, not fitted legal measurements.2. Problem / scientific context. Paper concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21910874 (this PDF 10.5281/zenodo.23262565, v1.2) defines the governance patch-gap as a rate ratio and a stock, and maps legal open texture onto HOT / robust-yet-fragile design. G in that PDF is a definition, not a dynamical model; the §4.4 powers of ten are heuristics. This software exists so those qualitative distinctions can be run.3. Contents. HOT vs uniform-null generators (simulation/graph.py); counted Rd, patcher, actor filters, two-stock dynamics (simulation/dynamics.py); thin Monte Carlo gate (verify_patchgap_claims.py); Lean 4 stock-algebra proofs (lean/, no Mathlib); regime-arithmetic, frozen 162-cell searcher sweep, source/prediction ledger (gates/, run_all_gates.py); five labeled SVGs; executed gate evidence under results/. No paper PDF. No real legal corpus.4. How to reproduce. Python 3.10+ (3.8 likely fine). No third-party packages. python verify_patchgap_claims.py — exit 0 iff three toy claims hold. python run_all_gates.py runs Lean (toolchain v4.15.0), regime arithmetic, searcher sweep, and source/prediction checks. Optional: python -m simulation.figures.5. Relation to paper. Illustrates the paper's §4.3 stock algebra and §4.4 distinction that G can fall while Sknown still rises, and that a searcher aimed at interfaces is not the same as a degree-greedy crawl of a robust core. Load-bearing legal argument, predictions, and named weaknesses stay in the PDF. Paper and software are separate Zenodo concepts; do not merge this zip with the paper record 21910874 / 23262565.6. Limitations / non-claims. Not a measurement of legal G, Rd, or Rp. Not support for the paper's heuristic exponents. Not a test that any statute is HOT (the generator assumes HOT structure). Not SocioHack, A1/VERITE, Rice/FLP, or an evaluation of any live bill. Claim 2 is the stock identity made executable. Claim 3 is an existence path under scenario knobs, not a legislature model.7. This version (v0.2.0, 2026-10-09). Adds four gates without changing the toy dynamics: Lean 4 proofs of the paper's §4.3 stock algebra (no sorry, standard axioms only; toolchain v4.15.0); a recomputation of the §4.4 regime arithmetic against the paper text; a 162-cell searcher sweep frozen before its first run (failed v1 spec kept); and a figure/prediction ledger that fails on drift or retired phrasing. Supersedes v0.1.1 10.5281/zenodo.21918637.8. Citation. Cite the paper concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21910874 for the argument.
