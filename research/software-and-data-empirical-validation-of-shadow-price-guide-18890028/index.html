Abstract

Source and analysis scripts for Empirical Validation of Shadow-Price-Guided Inlining in MIR (10.5281/zenodo.18828679). Three-phase pipeline: synthetic profiling across 750 function-call scenarios, a shadow-price mutation pass on MIR_CALL, and wall-clock benchmarking under baseline, uniform, skewed, and perturbed conditions. Language: C (MIR patches) and Python (analysis).
