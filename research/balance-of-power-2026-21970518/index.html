Balance of Power 2026

Bilar, Daniyel Yaacov · 2026-08-16 · software v2.13.2 · mit-license

Concept DOI (always latest): https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20370929
Source: github.com/chokmah-me/BoP2026

Abstract

A turn-based multipolar crisis simulation for IR research and war studies pedagogy. Eight scenarios across thirteen domains and eight actors; browser wargame and headless Oracle API for batch runs and counterfactuals. Stylized, rule-based AI with cascade dynamics.
